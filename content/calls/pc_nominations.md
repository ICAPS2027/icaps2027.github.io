# Call for Program Committee Self-Nominations

ICAPS 2027 invites researchers to nominate themselves for the Program Committee (PC). We welcome nominations from researchers working in automated planning and scheduling, as well as in related areas. Reviewers are needed for both the main track and the special track on [Planning Under a Different Name](/calls/special_tracks/), which covers planning-related work from neighboring AI areas such as reinforcement learning, natural language processing, robotics, and program synthesis.

Nominees should have at least one peer-reviewed publication in automated planning and scheduling or a related area, and some prior reviewing experience. PhD students who meet these requirements are welcome to apply.

## What PC Members Do
- Bid on submissions that match their expertise
- Review the papers assigned to them (long and short)
- Read the authors' responses and take part in the online discussion of their papers, led by a Senior PC member

Reviewing will be managed through EasyChair. All committee members are expected to keep submissions confidential and to follow the [ICAPS Code of Conduct](https://www.icaps-conference.org/icaps-code-of-conduct/).

## Reviewing Timeline
All deadlines are 11:59 PM UTC-12.
- Self-nomination deadline: November 26, 2026
- Bidding: December 9–15, 2026
- Reviews due: January 25, 2027
- Author response: February 4–8, 2027
- Discussion: February 9–19, 2027

## How to Apply
Please fill in the [self-nomination form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfU8l1oxIqDgl2cUEIKc2DpUdXZs3BgiUSAaqp3w1uiXJSz2w/viewform) by November 26, 2026. The program chairs will review the nominations and send EasyChair invitations to selected candidates at the email address given in the form.

For questions, contact: icaps-2027-organizers@googlegroups.com
