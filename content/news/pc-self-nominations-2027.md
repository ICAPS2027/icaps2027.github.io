**Interested in reviewing for ICAPS 2027?**

We invite researchers in automated planning and scheduling, and in related areas, to nominate themselves for the ICAPS 2027 Program Committee. Reviewers are needed for both the main track and the special track on Planning Under a Different Name.

See the [call for PC self-nominations](/calls/pc_nominations/) for details and the nomination form. Nominations are due November 26, 2026.
