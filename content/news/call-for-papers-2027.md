**The ICAPS 2027 Call for Papers is out!**

ICAPS 2027 invites submissions on all aspects of automated planning and scheduling. In addition to the main track, this year features a special track on [Planning Under a Different Name](/calls/special_tracks/), aimed at work rooted in neighboring AI communities that makes a substantive contribution to planning or deliberation.

Key dates (all deadlines at 11:59 PM UTC-12):

- Abstract submission deadline: December 7, 2026
- Paper submission deadline: December 14, 2026
- Author response period: February 1-4, 2027
- Author notification: February 26, 2027

See the full [Call for Papers](/calls/cfp/) for scope, submission categories, and author guidelines.
